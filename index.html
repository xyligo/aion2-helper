Aion 2 Crafting & Progression Guide

Essential mechanics, leveling tips, and Best-in-Slot (BIS) investment strategies.

⚒️ Proficiency Ranks

  • Novice (Lv. 1–50): Focuses on foundational gear, basic iron/steel metallurgy, and standard restorative consumables.
  • Promotion Quest (Lv. 50): At level 50, visit the Crafting Guild Master in Sanctum or Pandaemonium to undergo the Professional Daeva exam.
  • Professional (Lv. 51–100): Unlocks high-tier Titanium, Adamantium, Orichalcum, and Celestial recipes. Grants access to Transfer Crafting.
  • Proficiency Bonus: Having proficiency significantly above the recipe requirement boosts your Critical Proc (Noble) chance by up to +20%!

🌿 Essence Extraction (Gathering)

  • Universal Gathering: Essence Extraction combines mining, foraging, and organic harvesting into a single cohesive skill.
  • Aethertapping: Harvest floating Aether crystals in high-altitude flight zones and the Reshanta Abyss.
  • Rare Node Weather: Certain mythical ores and flowers (e.g., Dragon Flowers, Tears of Atreia) only bloom during celestial atmospheric shifts.
  • Flight Time Buffs: Always keep Flight Recovery Potions and Aerial Speed food active when harvesting Abyss Aether swarms.

✨ Critical Procs & Pity Tokens

  • Noble & Shining Procs: Crafted items have a chance to proc into enhanced "Noble" or "Shining" variants with superior stats, extra socket slots, and PvP damage multipliers.
  • Supply Request System: Normal (non-proc) crafts are never a total waste. They yield Pity Supply Tokens that can be exchanged for guaranteed masterwork catalysts.
  • Broker Market Dynamics: Selling intermediate refined ingots and elixirs often produces more reliable daily Kinah margins than gamble-crafting high-tier weapons.

💎 Transfer Crafting Mechanics

  • Gear Substat Retention: When upgrading to a higher gear tier, Transfer Crafting allows you to carry over your customized substats.
  • Amplification Retention: A +15 gear item retains +13 or +14 amplification when transferred, saving massive quantities of enchantment stones.
  • Catalysts: Stockpile Transfer Catalysts from dungeon breakdown and world boss drops.
